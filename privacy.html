Privacy Policy

Hefto: Rucking Tracker · Last updated September 28, 2026

Hefto tracks weighted walks (rucks). Your routes, rucks, body weight and pack weight stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There are no accounts, no server that stores your rucks, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your devices

  • Your rucks: time, distance, pace, climb, pack weight, ground and estimated calories.
  • Routes (GPS points) recorded while a ruck is running.
  • Your body weight, pack weight, units, goal and program progress.

Location

Hefto uses your location only while a ruck is running, to draw the route and measure distance, pace and climb. It keeps working with the screen off during a ruck (the blue location indicator shows). Location is never sent to us or anyone else.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you turn it on, Hefto saves each ruck to Apple Health as a walking workout with distance and active energy. On Apple Watch, Hefto reads heart rate and walking distance during a ruck. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your devices through Hefto. Rucks recorded on the watch are sent to the Hefto app on your paired iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Hefto receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier, purchase status and your first-run answers in general form (goal, pack weight, days per week) to validate purchases and measure how the paywall performs. It never receives your routes, rucks or health data.

Analytics and tracking

Hefto does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above. There are no ads.

Deleting your data

Swipe a ruck in the Log to delete it, or delete Hefto from your iPhone to remove everything it stored. Workouts saved to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.

Not medical advice

Calories in Hefto are estimates based on your weight, load, pace, grade and ground (the Pandolf load-carriage equation). They are not a medical measurement. Talk to your doctor before starting a new training program.

Children

Hefto is not directed at children under 13.
