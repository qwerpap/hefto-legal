Common questions

How are calories calculated? With the Pandolf load-carriage equation: your body weight, pack weight, speed, uphill grade and the ground you walk on. It's an estimate, and it counts the pack — which plain walking workouts don't.

My ruck stopped recording. Hefto saves an unfinished ruck every 15 seconds. Open the app and it continues from where it stopped. Make sure location for Hefto is set to "While Using the App".

How do I use Apple Watch? Install Hefto on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone, pick the pack weight with the Digital Crown and tap Start. The ruck appears in the Log on your iPhone.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Hefto.
